# 15-reviewer audit, 7 November 2026

10 reviewers looked for bugs, failures and business-model gaps; 5 looked at design. All read-only. This file lists
what was fixed (commits `9ecc50b`, `3128c42`) and what is still open, most important first.

## Fixed
- **Security:** the "door" (Lab vs app address) could be faked with an `X-Forwarded-Host` header; requests through
  Cloudflare now never count as this PC; the app port listens on this PC only (it was open to the whole Wi-Fi);
  staff could link their own Telegram as the owner's alert chat; webhooks could probe the inside network (now public
  addresses only, no redirects); sessions now signed with their own `SESSION_SECRET`; the public site now gets the
  security headers.
- **Wrong results:** Approve then Reject sent Approve; the approvals poll wiped the note being typed; the Inbox
  jumped back to All and lost the draft on every new message; a reply could be sent twice and a failed delivery
  looked sent; cancelling a booking or removing blocked time had no confirm and no error; the booking page's
  "manage" link showed the default business after a reload; a public booking with someone's phone showed the name
  on file; booking fields had no length limit and no booking window; unknown business ids crashed `/days` and
  `/slots`; coupons failed on their last day; a moved booking got no new reminder; a message saved just before a
  restart was never answered.
- **Lab:** a "new plan" with an existing name overwrote that plan; running cost ignored the real Claude spend;
  margin and return used list price for clients on trial; expired trials counted as pipeline; the Claude budget
  could not be edited; an upgrade could be approved twice or add a paid add-on for something already included.
- **Data:** Sent offers counted skipped sends; promotional automations went out without the Marketing add-on;
  no-shows counted as revenue in some charts.
- **Website:** the hero light animation never loaded for visitors.
- **Tests:** they no longer borrow the real admin, and the platform switch test always restores the switch.
- **Light mode:** see `app/public/light.css`.

## Fixed in the second group
- **Billing:** the Lab records payments (Whish, OMT, bank, cash, card) and moves "paid until" forward. A trial
  ends 3 days after its date, a paid plan 7 days after "paid until". When a plan has ended or is paused the AI stops
  and hands chats to the owner, online booking closes, and the dashboard is read-and-reply only. The owner sees a
  notice 3 days before. The owner's "pays for itself" figure uses the real plan price (not $79). Test businesses never end.
- **Messages:** Telegram and Instagram go through the restart-safe queue (one chat answered in order); every channel
  send gives up after 10 s; a reply that could not be delivered flags the chat in Needs you; the first reply of every
  chat says it is an AI assistant (EU AI Act); WhatsApp reminders outside the 24-hour window use an approved template
  (WA_REMINDER_TEMPLATE) or email, never free text. A short "Thanks!" no longer crashes the reply.

## Fixed in the third group (backups and privacy)
- **Backups:** checked every hour and made whenever the newest good one is older than 23 h, so a reboot or a PC that
  was off no longer skips a day; only automatic copies are cleaned up (manual-* and pre-* safety copies stay).
- **Off-site:** once a day the watchdog encrypts the newest backup (AES-256, key BACKUP_KEY) into OneDrive\MXAi backups.
  Checked: the copy decrypts to the identical file. OneDrive must be running to upload it.
- **Restore test:** once a week the newest backup is restored into a throwaway database and compared with live data.
  First run passed (every table restored). Lab › Health shows both.
- **Privacy:** erasing a client also removes their words from questions and sent messages; each client can keep chats for
  6-36 months (Lab); IP addresses in sign-in records are removed after 12 months; unhandled incoming messages after 7
  days; leftovers of closed businesses removed and now deleted with the business; the export contains every table.
- **One-time fixes** run once (schema_once), so a restart no longer moves erased clients' AI bookings to manual.

## Fixed in the fourth group (ease of use)
- **Today:** uses the business's own time zone; links open the right tabs (Answers, Auto-messages, Channels); when a
  check fails to load it says so instead of showing "All clear"; "Your team today" is now "Your helpers today".
- **Inbox:** dates work on old iPhones; clicking chats quickly no longer mixes them; a chat opened from Today has the right
  name and buttons (and can be handed back to the AI); a failed load shows Try again.
- **Calendar:** the newest week always wins; a touch that becomes a scroll cancels the drag.
- **Phone menu:** Today, Inbox, Calendar, Clients, notifications and More (every other page, light/dark, sign out).
  It fits a 375px phone with no sideways scrolling.
- **Names:** the AI page carries the assistant's own name; Delivery queue -> Messages not sent, Scenarios -> Practice
  chats, Versions -> Undo changes. Locks now cover Growth › Overview (Marketing) and To review / Brief (Teach).
- Still to do here: the assistant's saved shortcuts use old tab numbers; one-tap Confirm on Today.

## Still open (next, in this order)
1. **Money:** nothing is billed and trial end is not enforced; one USD price for Lebanon and Spain; the $79 default
   price matches no plan; Starter gets every channel (no channel gate). Decide pricing (business-model doc §3.3).
2. **Messages:** Telegram and Instagram can lose a message during a restart (not in the inbound queue); reminders sent
   more than 24 h after the client's last message need WhatsApp templates; channel sends have no timeout; failed
   sends are not flagged; no "I'm an AI" line in the first reply (EU AI Act, needed for Spain); Spanish fixed texts.
3. **Backups and privacy:** backups skip days (24 h sleep loop), are unencrypted and only on this PC, and a restore
   has never been tested; deleted businesses leave personal data in `ai_queue`, `learning`, `agent_actions`; no
   retention limit on messages and the audit log; the business export misses some tables; a startup fix-up can move
   AI bookings to manual after a client is erased (one-time migrations needed).
4. **Lab:** currencies are added together as dollars; "Resume AI for every client" also resumes clients you paused on
   purpose; deleting an add-on silently removes it from every client; closing a client keeps no copy.
5. **Dashboard UX:** Today links to some wrong tabs and shows "All clear" when a check failed; the AI has four names;
   39 hidden alias tabs; the phone menu does not fit (8 buttons); confirming a booking and sending an offer take 3-5
   taps; Today uses the phone's time zone instead of the business's; a chat opened from Today has the wrong header;
   calendar can show the wrong week on fast clicks and a touch drag can stick; dates may show "Invalid Date" on old
   iPhones; empty states give no next step; jargon (Delivery queue, Scenarios, Versions).
6. **Website:** no real proof (testimonials hidden, mock-ups not labelled "Example"); no price strip on the home
   page; no Arabic or Spanish; the hero should show a WhatsApp chat; one name per button; privacy page needs the
   company, a contact and the full provider list; SEO (per-page titles, canonical, og image, robots/sitemap);
   self-host the font; reduced-motion hides the hero chat; old screenshots.
7. **Front-end code:** 15 stylesheets (625 KB), 2,842 `!important`, 850 hard-coded colours. Consolidate step by step
   (tokens file, one button/input/card definition, merge theme layers, prune unused rules).
8. **Tests to add:** booking create/move/cancel, webhook signatures, campaign send rules, password/2FA/sign-out,
   public booking token, public API keys, admin user management, agents send, support extend/end, WeBook writes,
   time off; a browser check for JavaScript errors.
9. **Smaller security items:** the CSP still allows inline scripts; logout does not end the session on the server;
   the email code check is not constant-time; WeBook chat answers for unlisted businesses; rate-limit maps are
   never pruned.
