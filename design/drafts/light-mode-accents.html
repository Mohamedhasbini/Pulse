Light mode: accent options

Lime #c6f432 on the silver card is only 1.26:1 contrast (1.10:1 for the deeper #a6d11c). Charts, outlines and selected states need at least 3:1, and text needs 4.5:1. On graphite the same lime is 14.4:1, which is why it shines in dark mode and disappears in light mode.

The same components are shown in each option: tab bar, buttons, status chips, a KPI with a trend line, a bar chart with its peak, a heatmap, a progress bar and the live indicator.

1 · Chart lines and barsLime lines, sparklines and forecast lines fade into the silver card.
2 · Heatmaps and pivotsLime shading turns into a pale wash, so busy and quiet hours look the same.
3 · Selected statesLime outlines on the active tab, chosen option or current tile are almost invisible.
4 · Primary buttonsThe dark text on lime reads fine, but the button barely separates from a silver card.
5 · Live dots and the AI pillLime glows look like a stain on silver instead of a signal.
6 · Progress and goalsLime progress bars on a silver track are low contrast and look unfinished.
7 · Accent textLime text is unreadable on silver; it is already forced to graphite, which removes all emphasis.
8 · Brand consistencyAny fix must still feel like MXAi: graphite, silver and one sharp signal colour.
