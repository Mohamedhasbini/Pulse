Three colour directions

Brand name and logo

noora

Noora

“Light at your front desk.”

Arabic for light (نورة). Warm, easy in Arabic, English and Spanish, and it fits your AI receptionist, already called Nora. Mark: a ring with a light point, like a halo that's always on.

kairo

Kairo

“The right moment, every time.”

From kairos, Greek for the perfect moment: the right reply, the right slot, the right offer. Short and techy. Mark: a K with a timing dot.

velora

Velora

“Beauty runs itself.”

Soft and premium, feels made for salons, spas and clinics. Mark: a V like a sail filling with wind.

aurae

Aurae

“Your business, glowing.”

Aura, the glow around something. Pairs with the glass look. Mark: a core with expanding rings, like a signal.

My pick: Noora. It has meaning in your two markets, matches the AI's name, and works as a word people say ("ask Noora"). Before deciding, check the name and domain are free (trademark search and a .com/.ai domain). Pulse AI would become “Noora AI”.
