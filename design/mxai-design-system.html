MXAi · Dashboard design system · Proposal for approval · 3 Nov 2026

A calm operating system for a service business

The dashboard already holds everything a salon owner needs. The problem is order: the same number appears in four places, charts sit where work happens, and every card has its own size. This proposal fixes the foundation first: where things live, how big they are, what they say, and how they look in light mode. Nothing ships until you choose.

Five-second test today: fails

The owner sees 10 to 20 cards before the one thing that needs them. Today has 7 blocks plus a briefing, Insights had 45 chart types.

Biggest structural issue

Data lives by "what we could draw", not by "what the owner is doing". Revenue, AI activity and forecasts each appear on 3 to 5 pages.

Biggest visual issue

Four card sizes, four corner radii and three title styles grew up separately. No grid decides widths, so small cards float and big cards hold one number.

Recommended direction

Future Utility light mode: mist-grey canvas, crisp surfaces, graphite chips with the MXAi lime inside. It is the only direction that keeps the brand and dark mode as one family.

Information architecture

Deliverable 3

Component library

A design library, not a dashboard: each component has one business question, one size, the data states it can show and where it may be used. Pages pick from it; nothing is shown just because data exists.

Anatomy every component follows

  1. Title as a question or plain label: "Chats that became bookings", never "Conversion".
  2. Primary number: one figure, tabular digits, the largest text in the card.
  3. Context: what the number is out of or compared with ("of 42 chats this week").
  4. Trend: direction and size of change with a state colour, never colour alone.
  5. Explanation: one sentence on why it moved, from the data.
  6. Action (optional): one button that fixes or follows up.

Six data states

  1. healthy on target, no action needed
  2. improving moving the right way, show by how much
  3. neutral informational, nothing to judge
  4. warning needs a look this week, with the reason
  5. critical act today, always with an action button
  6. empty no data yet: say what will appear and how to start

Every state also has a loading skeleton, an error card ("Couldn't load bookings. Retry") and a phone variant.

Deliverable 4

Sizing and layout system

A 12-column grid on an 8px spacing scale. Four widget formats, three fixed heights. Content uses the full width up to 1680px, then centres, so large monitors get more breathing room, not wider cards.

Spacing

8px base: 4 for icon gaps, 8 inside rows, 16 between cards and inside card padding, 24 between groups, 40 between page sections.

Rows always fill

Each row's spans add to 12 (3+3+6, 4+8, 6+6). No lone compact card. If a card has no data it collapses into a one-line empty state, never a blank box.

Heights are fixed

104, 216 or 328px. Cards in one row share a height. Overflow scrolls inside the card with a "See all" link, so rows never stagger.

Breakpoints

≥1440: 12 cols, 24px gutters. 1024–1439: 12 cols, 16px. 768–1023: 6 cols (compact = 3, others = 6). <768: one column; compact cards pair two per row.

Type scale

12 label · 13.5 body small · 15 body · 17 card title · 22 section · 28 page title · 32–40 primary number. Two weights only: 400 and 600.

Progressive disclosure

Summary on the card, detail in a side drawer, everything else under "Explore" in Insights. Nothing opens a new page to show one more number.

Deliverable 5

Five light-mode directions

The same proposed Today screen in each direction. Switch between them; the token sheet and contrast checks under the preview update with it. Contrast is measured live against WCAG 2.2 (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and chart marks).

Deliverable 6

Recommendation: Future Utility

Future Utility is the strongest because it is the light half of the brand you already have. MXAi's dark mode is graphite with a rare lime signal; this light mode keeps graphite as the anchor and lets lime appear only inside graphite chips, where it reaches 14:1 contrast instead of failing on a pale background.

Its mist-grey canvas removes the glare and the flat black-and-white look of today's light mode, while the crisp off-white surfaces keep data sharp. It reads as precise and expensive, which matches an owner who runs a business from it.

  • Brand continuity: same graphite + lime family in both modes; switching modes feels like dimming the lights, not changing products.
  • Accessibility: every text pair passes 4.5:1; chart colours pass 3:1 on the surface.
  • Data clarity: a cool neutral canvas lets warning amber and critical red stand out without shouting.
  • Runner-up: Warm Mineral, if you want a warmer, more hospitality feel for salons and spas. It would need its own brand accent.

Phase 3 · Intelligence layer · 3–4 sessions

Recommendations and states

  • Automatic state colouring from thresholds (healthy, warning, critical)
  • "Why did this change?" sentences on trend cards
  • Remaining library components, used page by page as data allows
  • Goal tracking and benchmarks against the owner's own past
