How it is organised

Nothing is removed. Every chart, table and list Pulse has today is in this preview: click through the tabs. The rules:

1 · Each screen answers one questionMain tab = an area (Today, Dashboard, Clients…). Subtab = one question inside it ("What's happening now?", "Where do bookings come from?"). At most 4–5 blocks per subtab.
2 · Summary first, detail on demandCards show one number, a small chart or the top 3–5 rows. Full tables, long lists and filters open in a glass side drawer with the Open button, the same as the expand-and-CSV view you have now.
3 · Home stays calmHome is only what needs you today: the day, what's next, what needs you, three numbers. Everything else is one click away in Dashboard and Insights.
4 · Grouped by question, not by data typeDashboard's 10 subtabs become 5: Now, Conversations, Performance, Channels & services, Reviews. Insights' 8 become 5: Highlights, Money, Timing, Clients, Next steps. Old subtabs live on as sections inside them.
