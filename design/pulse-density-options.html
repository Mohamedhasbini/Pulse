Pulse · Dashboard
A · Tidy tiles. Every card is the same small size, holds one number and one tiny chart, and lines up on one grid. Nothing is wider or taller than it needs to be. Tables and big charts are not shown on the page: the buttons under the grid open them in the side panel.
  • Calmest and most even. Best for "glance and go".
  • Downside: you click once more to see a full chart.
B · Focus + list. The page shows one chart at a comfortable size, the one that matters most for this subtab. Every other number sits in a quiet list beside it, with a tiny trend line. Click a row and the big chart switches to it.
  • Easy to read: one thing to look at, everything else in a list.
  • Best balance between detail and calm. My recommendation.
C · Plain sentences. Each block starts with a sentence in normal words ("128 conversations this week, 18% more than last week"). The chart is folded under it and opens with one click.
  • Friendliest for owners who don't like charts.
  • Longer page; less useful when you want to compare numbers quickly.
