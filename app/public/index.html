Teach your AI

You're the boss of your assistant. Check its replies, fix anything it got wrong, and set rules it must always follow. Every correction makes it better.

Rules the AI must always follow

checked before every reply

Write them like you'd tell a new receptionist. Examples: "Never give discounts." "Always offer the 10:00 slot first on Saturdays." "Only answer in Arabic." "Don't book colour for new clients without a patch test."
