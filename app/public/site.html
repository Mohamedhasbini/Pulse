Not a chatbot. A team of AI agents that act.

Each MXAi agent senses what is happening, decides what to do, acts on its own and reports back to your command center. You set the rules. They do the work.

Sense

Reads every message, booking, cancellation and no-show across all channels, in real time.

Act

Decide

Chooses the best next step from your services, prices, hours, rules and each client's history.

Report

Every action lands in your dashboard with the result, so you know what happened and why.

Questions

Good to know

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp or Telegram like they always do. MXAi answers there.

What if the AI doesn't know the answer?

It says so politely and hands the chat to you, with an alert on your phone. You can reply from your dashboard and teach it the right answer for next time.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the customer's language, including English, Arabic (and Lebanese) and Spanish.

Can I keep using my calendar and team?

Yes. Add your team, opening hours and breaks. Bookings you add yourself are respected by the AI.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly subscription, cancel any time.
