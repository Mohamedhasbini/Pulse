Questions

Good to know

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp or Telegram like they always do. Pulse answers there.

What if the AI doesn't know the answer?

It says so politely and hands the chat to you, with an alert on your phone. You can reply from Pulse and teach it the right answer for next time.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the customer's language, including English, Arabic (and Lebanese) and Spanish.

Can I keep using my calendar and team?

Yes. Add your team, opening hours and breaks. Bookings you add yourself are respected by the AI.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly subscription, cancel any time.
