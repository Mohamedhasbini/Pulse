Privacy

Applies to Pulse (the AI assistant and dashboard for businesses), WeBook (the booking app) and each business's booking page. Last updated .

Who is responsible

Each business that uses Pulse decides why it collects its customers' data and is responsible for it (the controller). Pulse stores and processes that data only to run the service for the business (the processor). For data about business owners and website visitors, Pulse is the controller.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use one business's customer data for another business.

How long we keep it

Data is kept while the business uses Pulse. When a business closes its account, its data, including customers, messages, bookings and photos, is deleted. Backups are kept for up to 14 days and then deleted automatically.

How we protect it

  • Each business can only see its own data. This is tested automatically.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted bot keys, hashed passwords and optional two-factor sign-in.
  • Daily backups, and an activity log of sign-ins and important changes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or to stop marketing messages. Ask the business you booked with, or contact us and we will help within 30 days. If you are in the EU you may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

Contact

Use the form on the Pulse website and choose "Book a demo", writing "Privacy" in the message, or ask the business directly.
